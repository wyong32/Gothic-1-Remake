**Live site:** [https://gothic1remake.com](https://gothic1remake.com)

## About Gothic 1 Remake

You play a nameless convict dropped into the **Valley of Mines**, a penal colony sealed behind a magical barrier. Ore barons, mages, and novices fight for control while the Sleeper ritual looms in the background. There is no hand-holding: the journal names goals, not locations, and trainers only accept **ore** — not gold.

**Release date:** June 5, 2026  
**Platforms:** PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S  
**Developer:** Alkimia Interactive (THQ Nordic)

---

## What Makes the Remake Distinct

The Remake keeps the harshest design promises of the original while modernizing combat and world density:

| Feature | Gothic 1 Remake |
|--------|------------------|
| Navigation | No minimap, no quest markers — journal and NPC directions only |
| Progression | Strength, Dexterity, Mana, Learning Points; trainers paid in ore |
| Factions | Permanent three-camp choice: **Old Camp**, **New Camp**, **Swamp Camp** |
| Combat | Directional melee at its core, plus dodge, block, and lock-on after training |
| Story | Sleeper arc, focus stones, and Xardas pivot — expanded side content and orc lines |
| World | Larger, denser valley on UE5; professions from Gothic 1 removed |
| Audio | Kai Rosenkranz score re-recorded; German dub still treated as reference |

For a full side-by-side breakdown, see the [Remake vs Original guide](https://gothic1remake.com/guides/remake-vs-original).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is the Gothic 1 Remake release date?**  
June 5, 2026 worldwide on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

**Are professions still in the Remake?**  
No. Brewer, hunter, and alchemist job tracks from Gothic 1 were removed. Camp-specific side quests and expanded NPC routes replace that loop.

**Can I respec Learning Points?**  
No. Every LP spent at a trainer is permanent. Specialize one weapon line and one attribute early.

**How do I find quests without markers?**  
Listen to NPC dialogue, read journal entries, and navigate by landmarks. Use the [Interactive Map](https://gothic1remake.com/interactive-map) for route descriptions.

**Which camp is best for first-time players?**  
Old Camp offers the most Chapter 1 guidance. New Camp suits bow players. Swamp Camp is harder early but strong for late magic.

**Does the disc version need internet?**  
A one-time ~5 GB patch is required on launch day. After that, THQ Nordic confirms full offline play.

**Is this guide official?**  
No — it is a community walkthrough site. We update from public demos, developer interviews, and post-launch player reports. Contact: [wyong@gothic1remake.com](mailto:wyong@gothic1remake.com).
